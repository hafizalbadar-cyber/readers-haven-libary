Our story

A welcoming space for curious minds.

Readers' Haven Public Library helps people of every age find reliable information, enjoy great books and build lifelong learning habits. From a child's first story to a student's research project, there is something here for everyone.

Our mission is to make reading, knowledge and community resources accessible in a comfortable and inclusive environment.
