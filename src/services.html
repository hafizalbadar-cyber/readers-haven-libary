More than books

Tools and spaces
to help you thrive.

Use our learning facilities and digital resources to research, create and connect.

⌕

01 / Discover

Online catalogue

Search the library's collection by title, author or subject. Note down the book details and ask staff to help you locate it.

Available during library opening hours

▤

02 / Read anywhere

E-books

Explore digital reading resources on your personal device. Ask the library desk for access instructions and available titles.

Access guidance: Monday – Saturday

▦

03 / Focus

Study-room booking

Request a quiet study room at the help desk. Provide your preferred date, time and group size; bookings depend on availability.

Booking desk: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

▧

04 / Get it done

Printing and scanning

Bring your document on a USB drive or use an available device. Ask staff for current printing rates and scanning instructions.

Service hours: 9:00 AM – 6:30 PM

⌁

05 / Stay connected

Free Wi-Fi

Visitors can request the guest Wi-Fi details at the reception desk and use the connection for study and research.

Available in public reading areas
